# Measurement and growth review

Use the September 25 audit as the baseline. A low-volume site's weekly percentage changes can be misleading; record counts and use rolling 28-day comparisons. Exclude internal delivery tests.

## Weekly scorecard

Record date range, organic sessions, organic accepted inquiries, qualified commercial opportunities, assessments, quotes, wins and revenue. Include service and city, plus time to first response and leads with no next action. Source-to-win measurement requires the private CRM/register described in lead-operations.md; GA4 alone cannot establish revenue or lead quality.

For prospect reporting in GA4, use generate_lead as the sales outcome and inspect source/medium, landing page and service_interest. Keep support_request separate. Excluding a support pageview does not necessarily exclude an entire existing-client session. Do not label call or email clicks as confirmed conversations.

Check GSC impressions, clicks and index state for the homepage, managed IT, networking, security cameras, healthcare, New Albany, Tupelo, Oxford and service-area pages. Track Google-selected canonical and crawl dates. Investigate persistent exclusions before adding more pages. Record indexing requests separately from indexed status.
